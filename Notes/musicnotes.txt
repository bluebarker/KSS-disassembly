XX XX - index, unsure if used? First byte is subgame, second byte is stage number

XX XX - music, first byte is music number, second byte unsure but has to do with music

XX - Background display, for mode7 bosses (00 - no bg, 01 - show bg, 03 - ?)

XX - Graphics ratio, some maps and bgs may have more graphics than usual. (00 - use more bg graphics, 01 - default, 02 - use more tileset graphics)

XX - Background scrolling speed, top 4 bits Y, lower 4 bits X. (0 - stopped, 5 - same speed as the scroll of the map, 8 - max speed, 9 - automatically scroll when you call a specific object)

XX - Map scroll settings (01 - default, 02 - fixed screen, 05 - ?, 81 - left and right edges of the map loop, however, behavior is strange when an enemy touches a wall)

XX - Dark room (00 - default, 01 - dark)

XX XX - background position offset X and Y coordinates
